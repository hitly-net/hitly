title: Integrations
description: Wire Mastra, n8n, LangGraph, or Temporal to the Hitly inbox.
---

| Plugin | Status | Pause primitive | Resume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Mastra](/integrations/mastra) | Available | `suspend()` | `run.resume()` / `bail()` |
| [n8n](/integrations/n8n) | Coming soon | Wait webhook | POST `$execution.resumeUrl` |
| [LangGraph](/integrations/langgraph) | Coming soon | `interrupt()` | `HumanResponse` |
| [Temporal](/integrations/temporal) | Coming soon | `condition()` | signal `hitly.decision` |

The [envelope](/docs/envelope) is shared. Adding a framework means writing one adapter.
