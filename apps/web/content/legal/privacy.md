# Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Metaspan Ltd ("Metaspan", "we", "us") handles personal data when you use HITLy™ Cloud (cloud.hitly.net), our websites (hitly.net), our MCP server and API, the HITLy mobile apps for Android and iOS, and HITLy Support (support@hitly.net).

HITLy is a business-to-business service. It is not for consumers, and it is not for anyone under 18.

## 1. Who we are

Metaspan Ltd, trading as HITLy™, is registered in England and Wales (company number 13641200). Our registered office is 3 Saxon Way West, Corby NN18 9EZ, United Kingdom.

Privacy contact: support@hitly.net (please put "Privacy" in the subject line). You can also write to the address above. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because the law does not require one for our processing. The privacy contact handles these matters.

We have not appointed a representative in the EU under Article 27 GDPR. HITLy Cloud is provided from the UK and is not directed at people in the EU.

## 2. Two roles: controller and processor

**We are the controller** for data we use to run our business:
- your account and sign-in;
- billing, when billing launches;
- support requests;
- security and abuse logs;
- website and app analytics;
- business communications.

**We are a processor** for the content that our customers and their AI agents put into HITLy workspaces. That covers approval requests, prompts, questions, answers, decisions, evidence, comments, and project and workspace data. Our customer, usually your employer or the organisation that invited you, is the controller of that content. Our Data Processing Agreement (https://hitly.net/dpa) governs it, and we process it only on the customer's instructions. If you want to exercise rights over workspace content, contact that organisation first. We will help them respond.

**Self-hosted HITLy:** if your organisation runs the open-source HITLy server itself, Metaspan does not receive or process data from that installation. If the operator turns on the optional early-access waitlist form, entries go only to the webhook URL the operator sets. The form is shown only when public sign-up is turned off. The self-hosted inbox sends no usage data or telemetry to us.

## 4. AI and automated processing

- **No model training on your content.** We do not use workspace content, prompts, answers, evidence or support messages to train or fine-tune AI models.
- **No third-party AI in the product.** HITLy Cloud does not send workspace content to any third-party AI or large language model provider.
- **AI-assisted support, when launched.** When we launch AI-assisted support, AI support agents will help triage support emails, search our help articles, and draft replies. AI-assisted support is not built yet, so no AI model provider processes support emails today. If we use a third-party model provider when it launches, that provider would process the content of support emails, including any workspace content you choose to include. We will add it to our subprocessor list with 30 days' notice before it processes customer data. Higher-risk actions by AI support agents will need approval from a human at Metaspan. You will always be able to ask for a human to handle your request. AI support will not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.
- **No automated decisions.** Metaspan does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. Decisions that your organisation's AI agents route through HITLy are your organisation's decisions.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only cookies and device storage that are strictly necessary, or that remember choices you make, so we do not show a cookie banner.

| Item | Where | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sign-in session cookies (Better Auth, first-party) | cloud.hitly.net | Keep you signed in and protect against request forgery | Session, or until you sign out or the session expires |
| Security challenge cookies: `hitly.mfa_challenge`, `hitly.passkey_challenge`, `hitly.deletion_challenge` | cloud.hitly.net | Complete an MFA, passkey or account-deletion check you started | A few minutes, until the check finishes |
| Preference cookies: `hitly-theme`, `hitly-sidebar-collapsed` | cloud.hitly.net | Remember the theme and sidebar layout you chose | Up to 1 year, or until you change them or clear cookies |
| Workspace cookie: `hitly-workspace-id` | cloud.hitly.net | Remember the workspace you chose | Until you close your browser or sign out |
| Sign-in token stored securely on the device | Mobile apps | Keep you signed in to the app | Until you sign out or uninstall the app |
| Push token stored by the app | Mobile apps | Deliver the notifications you turned on | Until you sign out, turn notifications off, or uninstall the app |
| Stripe cookies (when billing launches) | Checkout and billing pages only | Fraud prevention and secure payment | As set by Stripe |

Our analytics (self-hosted Plausible) uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It does not track you across sites or build profiles. If we ever add any non-essential cookie or tracker, we will ask for your consent first.

## 7. Where your data is stored and international transfers

HITLy Cloud runs on servers owned and operated by Metaspan Ltd in a UK data centre (High Wycombe). The data-centre provider supplies space, power and network only and has no logical access to data and does not process data on our behalf. HITLy Cloud backups, our analytics and our support systems are kept on servers in the UK. HITLy Cloud backups are also copied to an off-site backup in the UK operated by Metaspan. Support system backups will include a UK off-site backup.

Some service providers are in the United States, including email delivery, mobile push notifications (Expo, with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android and Apple Push Notification service for iOS), and company email for support@hitly.net. Where UK or EU personal data goes to the US, we rely on:
- the UK–US Data Bridge and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified;
- otherwise, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum, and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

EU personal data can flow freely to the UK under the European Commission's adequacy decision for the UK.

If we ever restore from a backup, we re-apply account deletions made since that backup before the service resumes.

## 9. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask us to:
- give you a copy of your data (access);
- correct it;
- delete it;
- restrict how we use it;
- give it to you in a portable format;
- stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests (objection; for direct marketing, this right is absolute).

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

**How to make a request.** You can delete your account yourself, in the web app (see https://hitly.net/account-deletion). Before deleting your account, the Account page lets you download your profile and the contents of any workspace that will be deleted with it (**Export my data**). For a full copy of your data, or to use any other right, email support@hitly.net with "Data request" in the subject line. We may need to confirm your identity first. We reply within one month. For complex or numerous requests we can extend that by up to two further months, and if we do, we will tell you why within the first month.

**US residents.** You can ask to know, access, correct or delete your personal data, and you can opt out of sale or targeted advertising, although we do neither. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. We honour Global Privacy Control signals.

## 10. Complaints

Please contact us first, at support@hitly.net. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). If you are in the EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

## 11. Security

We protect personal data with measures appropriate to the risk. They include encryption in transit, access controls and multi-factor authentication. Our WAL-G and PBS backups are encrypted, and live data is protected by access controls. Summary measures are listed in Annex 2 of our DPA. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and tell affected people without undue delay, as the law requires.

## 12. Children

HITLy is for business users aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

## 13. Changes to this notice

We will post changes here and update the effective date. If a change is material, we will tell account holders by email or in the app before it takes effect.
