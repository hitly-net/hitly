# HITLy™ Acceptable Use Policy

This policy forms part of the HITLy Cloud Terms of Service (https://hitly.net/terms). It applies to everyone who uses HITLy Cloud, the API, the MCP server or the apps, including AI agents that act for a Customer.

## You must not use HITLy to:

1. Break the law, or help anyone else break it. That includes data protection, consumer protection, financial services, export control and sanctions law.
2. Run, approve or support any AI practice prohibited by Article 5 of the EU AI Act, or by equivalent laws. Examples include manipulative or exploitative techniques, social scoring, and untargeted scraping of facial images.
3. Store or send content that is unlawful, infringing, defamatory, harassing, or that sexually exploits children.
4. Submit special category data (such as health, biometric or genetic data, or data about racial or ethnic origin, religion, sexual orientation, political opinions or trade union membership), criminal offence data, or children's data, unless Metaspan has agreed to it in writing.
5. Send spam, phishing or malware, or use HITLy notifications or emails to contact people who have not agreed to be contacted.
6. Probe, scan or test HITLy's security without written permission. Report vulnerabilities through https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/security/advisories/new instead.
7. Get around rate limits, plan caps, authentication or access controls. Do not share accounts or API keys outside your organisation.
8. Disrupt or overload the Service, or interfere with other customers' use of it.
9. Reverse engineer HITLy Cloud, except as the law permits. The self-hosted server's source code is available under the Apache License 2.0.
10. Resell or sublicense HITLy Cloud, or offer it as a service to third parties, without our written agreement.
11. Point evidence sinks, webhooks or telemetry exporters at systems you are not authorised to use.

## Your responsibilities

- You remain responsible for your AI agents and for the actions they take after a human decision.
- Keep your credentials, passkeys and API keys secure, and revoke any that are exposed.
- Make sure that everyone whose personal data you put into HITLy has been told about it, as the law requires.

## Enforcement

If we reasonably believe this policy has been breached, we may:
- remove content;
- disable API keys;
- suspend or restrict accounts or workspaces;
- end the Terms.

We act in proportion to the breach and, where practical, warn you first. We may report illegal activity to the authorities.

## Reporting abuse

Report abuse to support@hitly.net, with "Abuse" in the subject line.
