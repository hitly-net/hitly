# HITLy weekly changelog — 19 November 2026

**Reporting window:** 12 November (after the previous edition) through 19 November 2026 (Europe/London).

The headline: HITLy Cloud is open for public sign-up. Sixteen pull requests merged to HITLy `main` this week, focused on account safety, privacy rights, plans and the legal groundwork for opening up. Everything listed below is on `main` and is now running on HITLy Cloud and hitly.net, except where a note says otherwise. There were no merged changes to the public HITL spec this week.

## Public sign-up, with legal pages

HITLy Cloud now accepts public sign-ups instead of a waitlist, and the sign-up form links to the terms and privacy notice. The approved legal texts are published as pages on hitly.net: privacy notice, terms, acceptable use policy, data processing agreement, subprocessors, account deletion and a legal index. The waitlist form also no longer sends submissions anywhere by default; a self-hosted install only forwards them if the operator configures a destination.

Sources: [HITLy #176](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/176), [#172](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/172).

## Verified email addresses

Public email and password sign-up now sends a verification link. Opening the link shows a Confirm button, so mail scanners that pre-fetch links cannot use up the token, and each link is tied to the address it was sent to. A separate setting lets a deployment limit what unverified accounts can do (such as creating projects) while still letting them sign in and verify; it is off by default.

Sources: [HITLy #160](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/160), [#164](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/164).

## MFA on every sign-in route

If you have multi-factor authentication turned on, every way of signing in now asks for your second factor, not just email and password. The exception is a passkey that already verified you on your device. Branded emails for passkey events and public brand assets that email clients can load were fixed at the same time.

Sources: [HITLy #161](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/161), [#163](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/163).

## Your data and your account

- **Delete my account.** Settings now has a full account-deletion flow: a summary of what goes with the account and which shared workspaces survive, an offer to request your data first, a fresh identity check and a typed confirmation. The deletion runs as a single transaction, with tests that prove it rolls back cleanly.
- **Request my data.** A "Request my data" button in your profile records a data request, and the confirmation states the one-month response deadline under UK GDPR. Our support team prepares and sends the export.

Sources: [HITLy #169](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/169), [#171](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/171), [#168](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/168).

## Plans, limits and pricing

Which features each plan includes, and numeric caps such as seats and retention, now live in one place that the app reads through a single helper. A plan change no longer needs a code change, and an unknown plan or limit is refused rather than allowed. The marketing pricing page now reads one shared pricing config, with GBP/USD and monthly/annual toggles and prices shown excluding VAT. This is groundwork only; it does not add payment processing.

Sources: [HITLy #166](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/166), [#170](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/170), [#174](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/174).

## Reliability and security housekeeping

- Rate limits are now shared across all app instances, so a limit holds however many servers are running. Raw IP addresses, emails and API keys are not stored in the limiter.
- hitly.net serves a standard `/.well-known/security.txt` that points security researchers to our private reporting channel.

Sources: [HITLy #167](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/167), [#165](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/165).

## Smaller changes

- The item detail page now has a single "View original request" panel (redacted JSON, hashes, copy and download) instead of two overlapping JSON views. [HITLy #173](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/173)
- The mobile app icon now sits inside Android's safe zone so it is no longer clipped, and web and Expo Go show the HITLy icon. This reaches devices with the next app build. [HITLy #158](https://github.com/hitly-net/hitly/pull/158)
